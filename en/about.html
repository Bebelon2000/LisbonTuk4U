About Guide Susane

Your certified host in Lisbon.

Hello! I'm Susane, your local guide in Lisbon. My passion for the rich history of Portugal's capital began at a very young age. For me, every monument, every viewpoint, and every cobblestone in Alfama hides a fascinating story that deserves to be told.

Over the years, I have perfected my tours to offer not just a traditional tourist itinerary, but an authentic journey through Lisbon's culture, gastronomy, and traditions. I love sharing curiosities about the historic neighborhoods and giving the best recommendations on where to listen to Fado or taste the local cuisine.

Aboard our 100% electric blue tuk-tuk decorated with flowers, I invite you to experience Lisbon in a sustainable, comfortable, and very fun way. With a meeting point at the Hard Rock Café or direct pick-up at your hotel, I guarantee an unforgettable circuit tailored to your pace.

I speak fluent Portuguese, English, Spanish, Italian, and French, ensuring close and natural communication throughout the tour.

Discover the charms and secrets of Lisbon aboard our iconic blue tuk-tuk decorated with flowers, guided by Susane.
