About Susane

Your local guide in Lisbon

A certified, warm host who is passionate about the city’s stories.

Hello! I’m Susane, your local guide in Lisbon. My passion for the history of Portugal’s capital began when I was very young. For me, every monument, every viewpoint and every cobblestone in Alfama hides a fascinating story.

Common questions

Questions before you book

Everything first-time bookers usually ask.

Where does the tour start?

We pick you up for free at your hotel or accommodation in Lisbon. If you prefer, we can arrange a meeting point in the city centre (next to the Hard Rock Café).

What is included in the price?

A 100% electric private tuk-tuk, a certified local guide with commentary in Portuguese, English, Spanish, Italian and French, and hotel pick-up. Monument tickets, meals and drinks are not included.

How many people fit in the tuk-tuk?

Up to 5 people per tuk-tuk. For groups of 6 or more we automatically book 2 tuk-tuks and the price doubles — calculated instantly, no surprises.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before the tour — no questions, no fine print.

How do I pay?

You pay online by card in a secure checkout (Stripe). The price is always per group and all-inclusive — no hidden fees.
